Screen design — Unit 2.0

One screen holds the whole app. The same markup is drawn three times below, at 360 px, at 768 px and at 1440 px. Nothing else exists at rest: the rule-set overrides, the theme builder and the roll log all sit behind the one more button.

  • The throw — six per-type steppers and the difficulty control. Each stepper is one tile with a minus end and a plus end. The difficulty control says what it will do before you roll.
  • On the table — the dice as they lie, and the history matrix beside them. One column per die, one row per generation. A kept die repeats its face down the column.
  • The action bar — the cost of the push is written above the button, so you read it before you commit. One tap rolls. One tap pushes.
  • The state shown is a pool of seven dice, rolled once and pushed once. The push added a stress die, so that column is blank in the first row.
