Tray at 25 dice — option E: Scatter, the camera pulls back

The dice lie where the physics left them. No grid holds them. The view widens until the whole spread fits inside it, so a larger pool means a smaller die. This is the most physical option and the least ordered one.

The state drawn is state B, after a throw. The pool holds the maximum of 25 dice: 15 pool dice and 10 stress dice. Thirteen dice are locked and twelve go back in the cup. The same screen is drawn three times below, at 360 px, at 768 px and at 1440 px.

  • It reads as a table with dice on it, which is the whole point of the app.
  • The lock state is drawn as a ring around the die, because a pad does not survive rotation.
  • The cost is the label and the height. A die carries no name, and the felt runs tall on a phone.
